Why I built this

I wanted more time with a fragrance before committing to a full bottle. I built Coast to make it easier to explore perfume houses and see how a scent wears through an ordinary day.

Frequently asked questions

How does shipping work?

Shipping availability, rates, and delivery times depend on the destination. This preview uses mock data, so live shipping quotes are unavailable.

What is a perfume decant?

A decant is a small amount of fragrance transferred from a full-size bottle into a sample vial or atomizer. The catalog shows 1 ml, 2 ml, 5 ml, and 10 ml options.

How can I check authenticity?

For an actual purchase, ask the shop about the source bottle and what documentation it can provide. The fragrances and perfume houses in this preview are fictional.
